Dialogue register (CRITICAL — applies to ALL novel styles, including literary):

- Use spoken sentence-final markers inside dialogue: တယ် / ပါတယ် / ဘူး / လား / လဲ / မလား / နော် / ပါ. Do NOT use literary သည် / ၏ / မည် / ပြီ as the closing verb form of a spoken line. (Literary endings are appropriate in narration, not in characters' mouths.)
